
# Tesla Demo Drive Hub: Achievements Guide

Welcome to the complete guide for every achievement in the Tesla Demo Drive Hub. This system is all about celebrating excellence, consistency, and the drive of our amazing advisors.

## Achievement Categories

Badges are split into categories, each focusing on a different part of performance and engagement.

---

### 🏆 Mastery & Milestones

This category is for the big moments in an advisor's journey, from their first drive to becoming a seasoned pro.

-   **First Drive Conductor**: Complete your first demo drive.
-   **Novice Advisor**: Rack up 10 total demo drives.
-   **Trusted Guide**: Hit 30 total demo drives.
-   **Product Expert**: Complete 50 total demo drives.
"Elite Ambassador**: Crush 100 total demo drives.
-   **Trifecta**: Knock out 3 demo drives in a single day.
-   **Pentathlon**: Dominate with 5 demo drives in a single day.
-   **Consistency is Key**: Complete at least one demo drive every day for two straight weeks.
-   **Model Maestro**: Drive every Tesla model your store has to offer.
-   **Fleet Familiarizer**: Get behind the wheel of 5 different vehicles (unique VINs).
-   **VIN Vanguard**: Take the keys to 15 different vehicles (unique VINs).

---

### ⭐ Performance & Excellence

This category is for top-tier work, an eye for detail, and giving customers an amazing experience.

-   **Punctual Pro**: Keep it tight with less than 5 overdue check-ins for two weeks straight.
-   **The Perfect Handover**: Bring a vehicle back with over 80% battery for 10 drives in a row.
-   **Feedback Champion**: You're a feedback magnet, logging customer comments on 90% of your check-ins.
-   **Issue Spotter**: Your sharp feedback flagged a vehicle for maintenance.
-   **Insightful Advisor**: You've received 5 customer comments that were more than two sentences long.
-   **Fleet Doctor**: You've flagged 5 separate vehicles for issues.
-   **Zero to Smile**: Logged feedback where a customer said the acceleration was "fun," "amazing," or "insane."
-   **Efficiency Expert**: Keep your average demo drive time in the sweet spot (e.g., 30-45 mins) for over 50 drives.

---

### ⚡ Tenacity & Hustle

This category is for advisors who bring the heat and go the extra mile.

-   **Weekend Warrior**: You owned the weekend by conducting the most demo drives.
-   **Early Bird**: Get a demo drive done and dusted before 11:00 AM.
-   **Night Owl**: Finish the last demo drive of the day after 7:00 PM.
-   **Marathon Driver**: Power through 5 or more demo drives in a single day.
-   **Lunch Rush**: Conducted a drive during the peak lunch hour (12 PM - 2 PM).
-   **First of the Month**: First advisor to complete a drive on the first day of the month.

---

### 🛡️ Knowledge & Evangelism

This category celebrates deep product knowledge and the skill to educate and inspire.

-   **Electric Ambassador I**: Lead 10 EV education-focused drives.
-   **Electric Ambassador II**: Lead 25 EV education-focused drives.
-   **Electric Ambassador III**: Lead 50 EV education-focused drives.
-   **Feature Guru**: Logged feedback mentioning a specific feature like Autopark, Dog Mode, or Summon.
-   **Tech Talk**: Use the mobile app to precondition or unlock a vehicle for a customer.
-   **Autopilot Authority**: Deliver a clear, safe, and mind-blowing Autopilot/FSD demo.
-   **Matchmaker**: You found the perfect match! A customer bought the exact car they drove.

---

### 🧩 Quirky & Fun

A collection of fun, unique badges for those unexpected moments.

-   **Lucky Charm**: A random, surprise badge awarded to one advisor per week. You got it!
-   **The Tour Guide**: That was a long one. You conducted a demo drive that lasted over 90 minutes.
-   **Quick Dip**: In and out! You wrapped up a mini-demo in under 20 minutes.

---

### ⚙️ Operational Excellence

This category rewards advisors who keep the demo fleet running like a well-oiled machine.

-   **Scheduler**: Master the system by booking 10 future demo drives.
-   **Team Player**: Check out a vehicle just to move it for charging. Thanks for helping out!
-   **Peak Performer**: You're the MVP, conducting the most drives during the store's busiest hour of the month.
-   **Power Nap**: Took a vehicle for an overnight charging session.
-   **Inventory Master**: Proactively check the status of every vehicle at the start of your shift.

---

### 💎 Elite & Secret

These are the rarest and toughest achievements, reserved for the most dedicated and observant advisors. The unlock criteria are a mystery.

-   **Flawless Week**: Completed 15+ drives in a week with zero negative feedback and few overdue check-ins.
-   **The Architect**: Your feedback directly led to an improvement or new feature in the Hub. This is manually awarded.
-   **Clairvoyant**: Be the first person at your store to drive a new model or trim on the day it's added to the fleet.
-   **Current Keeper**: Played it safe by taking a vehicle for charging when its battery was still above 70%.
-   **Goldilocks Driver**: Your average drive time was *just right*-between 40 and 70 minutes for a whole month.
-   **Hub Master**: The ultimate prize. You've earned every single non-secret achievement in the Hub.
