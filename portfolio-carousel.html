The hook

25 min

That is how long one availability check took.

Customer waiting. Whiteboard in the back. Group chat blowing up.

That Saturday rush is where this project started.

The leak

57 min

Real average demo time. We assumed 30.

That 27-minute gap explained double-bookings, long waits, and stressed advisors on weekend lunch rush.

You cannot fix a leak you never measured.

What I built

A mobile-first PWA for the showroom floor

  • Live fleet dashboard with 4 view modes
  • Check-in/out in under 30 seconds
  • Demo-to-sale + FSD attach tracking
  • Keyword maintenance flagging (no paid AI)
  • 50 achievements, 99-level XP, chart builder export
  • Fleet attention strip + optional live telemetry

From the floor

"Before this tool, I had to run to the office to check the whiteboard. Now I just check my phone while walking the guest to the car. It makes me look more professional." Senior Sales Advisor · pilot user feedback

Lesson 1

Speed wins on the sales floor

Advisors do not hate new tools. They hate slow ones.

Once fleet status was one tap and check-in took 30 seconds, usage climbed fast.

Lesson 2

Adoption is part of the product

The pilot worked. Advisors used it. Drives got logged.

Without org sponsorship, adoption plateaued. I turned the build into a portfolio showcase instead of rewriting the ending.

Lesson 3

Data ends arguments

Structured logs surfaced weekend peaks, real drive duration, top performers, and maintenance issues before the next customer sat down.

Clean data is how you stop guessing.
